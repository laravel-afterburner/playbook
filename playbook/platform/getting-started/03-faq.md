## Overview

The **FAQ** page (`/help/faq`) provides quick answers to frequently asked questions. It complements the full documentation in Help & Support — use FAQ for short, curated answers; use the sidebar guides for step-by-step workflows.

## Who can view FAQ

| Permission | Access |
|------------|--------|
| **View Playbook FAQs** | Read published FAQ entries |
| **Manage Playbook FAQs** | Create, edit, reorder, publish, and delete FAQ entries |

These permissions are separate from **View Help & Playbook** (documentation access). Assign them under the **Help** permission group in [Managing roles](/help/platform/managing-roles).

Members without FAQ permissions do not see the **FAQ** link in the Help sidebar. When no published FAQs exist, the link may also be hidden.

## Managing FAQ entries

Members with **Manage Playbook FAQs** permission can:

1. Open **FAQ** from the Help & Support sidebar.
2. Click **Add FAQ** to create a question and answer.
3. Toggle **Published** to make an entry visible to users with view permission.
4. Reorder entries with the move up/down controls.
5. Edit or delete entries as needed.

Draft entries are visible only to managers until published.

## System administrators

System administrators can manage FAQs even when team RBAC is not configured, and can access the FAQ page when no published entries exist yet.
