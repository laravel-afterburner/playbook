## Main navigation

After signing in, the top navigation bar provides access to the dashboard and feature areas registered for your application. Available items depend on your role and which packages are installed.

## {{ entity_label_title }} switcher

Use the {{ entity_label }} switcher in the navigation bar to change your active {{ entity_label }}. Many pages are scoped to your current {{ entity_label }}, including members, settings, and feature modules.

## Profile menu

Open your profile menu (top right) to access:

- **Profile** — name, email, and account details
- **Security** — password, two-factor authentication, and passkeys
- **Notifications** — in-app notification center

## Playbook

Open **Playbook** from your {{ entity_label }} menu to return to this guide at any time.

## Dashboard

The dashboard is your home base after login. It surfaces action items and links to areas that need your attention.
