## Overview

Roles control what members can do within a {{ entity_label }}. Each member is assigned one or more roles for the {{ entity_label_plural }} they belong to.

## Default roles

Applications ship with role templates appropriate to the domain. For example, a strata application may include President, Secretary, Council Member, and Owner roles.

## Permissions

Each role grants a set of **permissions** — discrete abilities such as managing meetings, viewing documents, posting announcements, or managing finances.

Permissions are grouped by area (Properties, Finances, Voting, Documents, Help, and others). Many groups include **bundle** permissions that grant a full set of related abilities — for example **Manage Finances** includes view access to council finance sections. Individual permissions below a bundle can be assigned separately when you need tighter control.

## Roles management page

Open **Roles** from your {{ entity_label }} menu to review default and custom roles, assign permissions, and configure options such as:

- **Role hierarchy** — drag to reorder; higher roles may manage lower roles when supported
- **Maximum members** — cap how many people can hold a custom role
- **Directory: Council** — flag a role so members appear in the council section of the [resident directory](/help/directory/member-directory)
- **Badge colour** — how the role appears in member lists

See [Managing roles](/help/platform/managing-roles) for the full walkthrough.

## {{ entity_label_title }} owners

The {{ entity_label }} owner always has full access within that {{ entity_label }}, regardless of assigned roles.

## Changing a member's role

Open the **Members** (or **Directory**) page, select a member, and update their assigned role. Changes take effect immediately.
