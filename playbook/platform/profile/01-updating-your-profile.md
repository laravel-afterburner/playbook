## Profile page

Open **Profile** from the profile menu to update your name and email address.

## Email changes

Changing your email may require re-verification depending on application settings. Check your inbox for a verification link after saving.

## Timezone

When timezone management is enabled, set your preferred timezone on the Profile page. The application uses this for displaying dates and may suggest a detected timezone when you travel.

## Directory contact (strata)

When your strata uses the resident directory, the Profile page may include per-{{ entity_label }} contact fields — phone, contact preference, and emergency contact — that control what other members see in Help & Support guides for your application.

## API tokens

When the API feature is enabled, create and revoke personal access tokens at `/user/api-tokens`. See [API tokens](/help/platform/api-tokens).

## Account deletion

If account deletion is enabled, you can permanently delete your account from the Profile page. This action cannot be undone.
