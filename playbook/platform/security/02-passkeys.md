## Overview

Passkeys (WebAuthn) let you sign in with your device's biometric sensor or security key instead of typing a password.

## Registering a passkey

1. Open **Security** from the profile menu
2. Add a new passkey
3. Follow your browser or device prompts to complete registration

## Signing in with a passkey

On the login page, choose the passkey option and authenticate with your device.

## Managing passkeys

Remove passkeys you no longer use from the Security page. Keep at least one sign-in method available.
