## Email and password

Visit the login page and enter your email address and password. If email verification is enabled, you must verify your email before accessing the application.

## Forgot your password?

Use the **Forgot your password?** link on the login page. You will receive a reset link by email.

## Passkeys

If your administrator has enabled biometric authentication, you can sign in with a passkey after registering one from your Security page.

## Session security

For your protection, sign out when using a shared device. You can also review and revoke active browser sessions from the Security page.
