## Accepting an invitation

When you are invited to a {{ entity_label }}, you receive an email with a signed invitation link. Click the link while signed in (or create an account first), then accept the invitation.

You can also accept invitations from your **Notifications** page when an in-app notification is sent.

## Declining an invitation

If you do not wish to join, decline the invitation from the notification or invitation page. The invitation is removed and the inviter is notified.

## Invitation expiry

Invitation links are signed and may expire. Ask a {{ entity_label }} administrator to resend the invitation if a link no longer works.

## After joining

Once you accept, switch to the new {{ entity_label }} using the {{ entity_label }} switcher. Your available permissions depend on the role assigned to you.
