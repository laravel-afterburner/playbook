## Overview

**Product Roadmap** (strata deployments) gives system administrators a view of shipped features and planned enhancements. It helps support staff answer “is this available yet?” without digging through release notes.

## Opening the roadmap

System administrators open **Product Roadmap** from the profile menu under System Administration.

## What it shows

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Available** | Features currently live in the application |
| **Planned** | Enhancements on the roadmap (admin context may include additional detail) |

The public welcome page may show a subset of roadmap information to visitors when configured.

## Using it with Help & Support

When users ask about upcoming capabilities (for example maintenance quote notifications or enhanced comparison tools), check the roadmap before promising timelines. Link them to relevant [Help & Support](/help/platform/welcome) guides for what is available today.
