## Overview

Announcements broadcast a message to all members of a {{ entity_label }}. Members receive an in-app notification and may receive email depending on configuration.

## Who can post

Members with the **post announcements** permission (or {{ entity_label }} owners) can create announcements.

## Creating an announcement

1. Open **Announcements** from the {{ entity_label }} menu
2. Create a new announcement
3. Enter a title and message
4. Publish immediately or schedule for later (if scheduling is enabled)

## Scheduled announcements

When scheduling is available, choose a future date and time. Scheduled announcements are sent automatically when the time arrives.

## Best practices

- Use a clear, descriptive title
- Keep messages concise; link to Documents for lengthy attachments
- Avoid posting sensitive personal information in announcement bodies
