## Overview

The notification center collects in-app notifications from across the application — invitations, announcements, meeting updates, ballot events, and more.

## Opening notifications

Click **Notifications** in the profile menu. Unread items are highlighted; action-required items may show a badge count in the navigation bar.

## Marking as read

Open a notification to mark it read, or use **Mark all as read** to clear the unread state for non-action items.

## Action-required notifications

Some notifications require a decision — for example, accepting or declining an {{ entity_label }} invitation. These remain prominent until you act on them.

## Email delivery

Many notifications also send email. Email delivery is configured at the application level; the notification center is always the in-app record of what was sent.

## Deleting notifications

You can dismiss individual notifications when you no longer need them. Dismissing does not undo the underlying action (such as an invitation you already accepted).
