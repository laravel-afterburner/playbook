## Overview

**System Settings** is the {{ entity_label }} administration hub for optional packages and application-specific configuration. Only members who can update the {{ entity_label }} (typically owners and designated administrators) see this item in the {{ entity_label }} menu.

## What appears here

Settings panels are registered by installed packages. Your {{ entity_label }} may include some or all of the following:

| Area | Typical configuration |
|------|------------------------|
| **Documents** | Folder structure, retention tags, upload limits |
| **Events / Voting** | Meeting defaults, ballot rules, proxy voting |
| **Finances** | Currency, fee amounts, late fees, reminders |
| **Public landing** | Welcome page contact email and council contact form (strata apps) |
| **Owners package** | Compiled PDF package for new owners (strata apps) |

Panels you do not see are either not installed or not enabled for your application.

Strata-specific guides:

- [Public landing page](/help/administration/public-landing)
- [Owners package](/help/administration/owners-package)

## Before changing settings

- Confirm the impact with other council or staff members when a setting affects billing, voting, or member access.
- Document significant changes in meeting minutes or council records when required by your bylaws.
- After changing finance or fee settings, review related Help & Support guides under **Finances** before generating assessments.
