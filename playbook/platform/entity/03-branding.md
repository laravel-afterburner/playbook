## Overview

{{ entity_label_title }} branding customizes how the application looks for members of your {{ entity_label }}. This typically includes a logo and accent colors.

## Updating branding

1. Open **Details** or **System Settings** for your {{ entity_label }}
2. Upload a logo (recommended square or landscape image)
3. Choose brand colors if available
4. Save your changes

## Favicons

Some applications generate favicons from your {{ entity_label }} logo automatically. Members may see your branding in the browser tab when working inside your {{ entity_label }}.

## Who can change branding

Only members with permission to update {{ entity_label }} settings can change branding. This is usually the owner or administrators.
